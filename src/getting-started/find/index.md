# Find Your Data

Once your data is in Entu, getting it back out is the easy part.

<!-- screenshot: toolbar search box with results from several entity types -->

## Search everything from one box

The search box in the toolbar looks through **all** your data at once — you don't have to know whether something was filed as a book, a document, or a contact. Type a name, a word from a description, anything you remember.

## Browse with the menu

The menu on the left groups your data into lists — for example **Books**, **Contacts**, **Invoices**. Each menu item shows a filtered list, so the things you use most are always one click away.

## List or table — your choice

A toolbar button switches between two views:

- **List view** — a compact list with the selected entry's details next to it. Good for reading and editing one thing at a time.
- **Table view** — all entries as rows with their fields as columns, like a spreadsheet. Good for scanning and comparing many entries.

<!-- screenshot: the same data shown in table view -->

## Every change is remembered

Each entry keeps its full history — who changed what and when. If something looks off, open the entry's history from the toolbar and see exactly what happened.
