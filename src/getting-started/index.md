# Create Your Account

You can try Entu in a couple of minutes. There is nothing to install and no password to invent — you sign in with an account you already have.

<!-- screenshot: sign-in page of entu.app with the list of sign-in options -->

## 1. Choose how you sign in

Go to [entu.app](https://entu.app/new) and pick the option that suits you:

- **Google or Apple** — one click if you are already signed in on your device.
- **Email** — we send you a link; clicking it signs you in. No password needed.
- **Smart-ID, Mobile-ID, or ID card** — Estonian e-identity options.

Entu never stores passwords, so there is no password to forget or leak. You can add more sign-in methods later — they all lead to the same account. Read more under [Authentication](/overview/authentication/).

## 2. Create your database

When you sign up, Entu creates a **database of your own** — a private space that holds your data. Only you can see it until you invite someone else.

## 3. Start from a template

You don't have to design anything from scratch. Pick a starter template — **contacts, documents, library, or inventory** — and you get a working structure you can start filling right away. Everything a template sets up can be changed later, so it's a starting point, not a commitment.
