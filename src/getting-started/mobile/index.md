# Entu on Your Phone

Your data is not tied to one computer. Entu works wherever you are.

## In any browser

[entu.app](https://entu.app) works in every modern browser and adapts to small screens — nothing to install. Sign in on any device and your data is there. This is also the way to use Entu on Android.

## The native app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

For Apple devices there is a native Entu app — the same account and the same data, with a faster, more at-home feel.

<!-- screenshot: iPhone frame with the Entu app showing an entity list -->

[Download it from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id1520575542?pt=120355100&ct=www&mt=8) — one app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Handy away from your desk

The phone is often exactly where Entu is most useful:

- Check whether you already own a book — while standing in the bookshop.
- Snap a photo of a receipt and attach it to the purchase right away.
- Look up a serial number in the basement, not back at your desk.
